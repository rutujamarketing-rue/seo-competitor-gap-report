# Prioritisation: Impact × Effort

Every opportunity in the report gets two scores (1–5) and a priority.

## Impact (1–5)

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | High volume, commercial/transactional intent, 3+ competitors rank, or a striking-distance query with high impressions |
| 4 | Solid volume and commercial intent, 2+ competitors rank |
| 3 | Moderate volume or mixed intent |
| 2 | Low volume or mostly informational |
| 1 | Marginal |

Weight intent: transactional/commercial ×1.0, informational ×0.6, navigational (competitor brand) ×0.2.

## Effort (1–5)

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | On-page fix (title, H1, schema, internal links) |
| 2 | Expand an existing page, KD < 20 |
| 3 | New page, KD 20–40, few links needed |
| 4 | New content hub or KD 40–60, 10+ new referring domains needed |
| 5 | KD 60+, authority gap > 30 points |

## Priority

| Priority | Rule |
|---|---|
| **P1 – Quick win** | Impact ≥ 4 and Effort ≤ 2 |
| **P2 – Strategic bet** | Impact ≥ 4 and Effort ≥ 3 |
| **P3 – Fill-in** | Impact ≤ 3 and Effort ≤ 2 |
| **P4 – Park** | Impact ≤ 3 and Effort ≥ 3 |

The 90-day plan schedules P1 in weeks 1–4, starts P2 in weeks 3–12, slots P3 in where there's capacity, and lists P4 as "revisit next quarter".
