# Report Template

```
# SEO Competitor Gap Report: <Your brand> vs <N> competitors
Focus: <topic / page> · Market: <country> · Data pulled: <date>

## 1. Executive summary
- Bottom line (2–3 sentences)
- Top 5 opportunities with priority (P1/P2)

## 2. Competitor scorecard
| Domain | Semrush AS | Ahrefs DR | Organic traffic (Semrush / Ahrefs) | Keywords | Referring domains | Focus-page rank |

## 3. Your real performance (Search Console, last 90 days)
- Clicks, impressions, CTR, avg position for the topic
- Striking-distance queries (pos 8–20)
- High-impression / low-CTR queries

## 4. Keyword gap
- Keyword universe: volume, KD, CPC, intent
- Consensus gaps (3+ competitors rank, you don't)
- Keywords you own that competitors don't

## 5. Content gap
- Competitors' top pages by traffic, grouped by topic
- Topics and formats you're missing (guides, comparisons, pricing, tools)

## 6. SERP analysis
- Top 10 for the main keyword: domain, page type, authority
- Intent mix and what a winning page must include

## 7. On-page comparison
| Element | Your page | Best competitor | Recommended |

## 8. Backlink gap
- Referring domains linking to 2+ competitors but not you
- Link quality note (what was excluded and why)
- Link-building targets by tactic

## 9. AI search visibility (optional)
- Mentions / citations / share of voice vs competitors

## 10. Opportunity matrix
| Opportunity | Data point | Impact | Effort | Priority |

## 11. 90-day action plan
| Weeks | Actions | Owner | KPI |

## 12. Methodology & sources
- Tools, databases, date ranges, row limits
- Where Semrush and Ahrefs disagreed
- Limitations
```
